# common questions
Q What's the difference between the three courses?
A Awareness (2h) covers basics for general staff, OSHA/EPA only. Property Management (4h) adds tenant communication, documentation, and OSHA/EPA/IAC2-aligned compliance. Mold Inspector Certification Training (6h) is IAC2-aligned for professional inspectors.

Q How much does mold training cost?
A $59.99 (Awareness), $229.99 (Property Management), $329.99 (Mold Inspector Certification Training) per seat. Bulk seat discounts at 2-10/11-20/21-50/51-100/101-250 seats.

Q Are the courses available in Spanish?
A Yes, all three courses have an in-course English/Spanish toggle.

Q Do I get a certificate?
A Yes, a certificate of completion is issued immediately on completion. This is not a state license or third-party professional certification.

Q Is this course state approved, EPA certified, or OSHA certified?
A No. EPA does not certify mold inspectors or remediation firms; OSHA does not issue mold-specific certifications; state approval is not claimed. Courses are designed to align with applicable EPA guidance, OSHA safety principles, and IAC2 standards where stated per-course. See /state-mold-training-requirements/ for state-by-state licensing context.

Q What standards do these courses follow?
A Federal OSHA and EPA guidelines for all three; the Property Management and Mold Inspector Certification Training courses are additionally aligned with IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards of Practice.

Q Where can I verify a certificate or view accreditation details?
A Certificate verification: https://hazwoper-osha.com/certificate-verification (direct link, not a moldtraining.us page). Certifications & accreditations: https://moldtraining.us/#certifications (routes to HAZWOPER OSHA Training's accreditation details, including IACET provider status).

Credential Transparency

What You Actually Receive

"Certificate," "certification," and "license" are not interchangeable. Here's what each term means so you know exactly what you're getting before you enroll.

Certificate of Completion

Documents that you finished a specific course. This is what moldtraining.us issues.

Training Certification

A credential showing you completed training aligned with a named standard (e.g. IAC2), not a license to practice.

State License

Legal permission to perform mold assessment or remediation in a given state, issued by that state's regulator, not by us.

Third-Party Professional Certification

A credential from an independent body (e.g. IICRC, NORMI, ACAC) with its own exam and renewal requirements.

Upon successful completion, students receive a certificate of completion for the selected mold training course. This certificate documents training completion and may support employer compliance files, internal training records, or continuing education needs. State licensing requirements vary, and this course may not replace state-required mold assessor or mold remediation licensing where applicable. See our State Mold Training Requirements page for details.

Disclaimer → EPA does not certify mold inspectors or mold remediation firms. OSHA does not issue mold-specific certifications. Our courses are designed to align with applicable EPA guidance, OSHA safety principles, and relevant industry practices.
