# moldtraining.us/state-mold-training-requirements/: agent-readable view

# summary
Mold licensing requirements vary by state. Several states (TX, NY, FL, LA, DC) require a state-issued license to
perform mold assessment and/or remediation for compensation. Most other states have no mold-specific license.
moldtraining.us courses are NOT currently part of any state's approved-provider list for licensing purposes.
They support awareness, documentation, prevention, and general training only.

State Mold Training & Licensing Requirements

Mold Licensing Varies by State: Check Yours First

Several states require a state-issued license to perform mold assessment or remediation for compensation. moldtraining.us courses are intended for awareness, prevention, documentation, and general training. They may not satisfy state licensing requirements. Check with your state regulator before enrolling for a licensing purpose.
