At a glance → This is the remediation-focused version of our 2-hour Mold Prevention & Remediation Awareness Training: cleanup methods, containment, PPE, and remediation guidelines for small, medium, and large contamination areas, plus when a licensed remediation contractor is required. It awards a certificate of completion valid 36 months. It is not an EPA certification, OSHA certification, or state contractor license — see state licensing requirements if you perform remediation professionally.

Who It's For

For Anyone Who Responds to Mold, Not Just Reports It

Take This Course If You're

Maintenance staff, facilities teams, or property personnel who clean up small-to-moderate mold issues directly, or who supervise remediation vendors and need to know what proper containment and cleanup should look like.

Consider Another Course If You're

Conducting professional inspections and sampling (see Mold Inspector Certification Training), or need broader tenant-documentation coverage (see Property Management Mold Training).

Not a Substitute For

A state contractor license where required. Some states (Florida, Texas, New York, among others) license professional mold remediation work separately — see state requirements.

Curriculum

Remediation-Focused Course Modules

5 lessons, taken in sequence, followed by a final examination.

  1. 01

    What is Mold?

    Mold basics, where it's found, how it grows, and health effects associated with exposure.

  2. 02

    Moisture Problems and Preventive Maintenance

    Common causes and hidden locations of moisture problems, and preventive maintenance for buildings and HVAC systems.

  3. 03

    Building Investigation and Remediation Plan

    Identifying contamination, assessment equipment, mold sampling trade-offs, and the key steps of a remediation plan.

  4. 04

    Mold Cleanup and Containment

    The three common mold cleanup methods, why biocides aren't recommended for routine use, containment concepts, and PPE.

  5. 05

    Mold Remediation Guidelines

    Area-specific remediation guidelines, cleanup procedures by contamination size, confined-space considerations, and why remediation evaluation requires professional judgment.

  6. ✓

    Final Examination

    A final knowledge check must be passed to receive your certificate of completion.
