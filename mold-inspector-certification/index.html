At a glance → This 6-hour, self-paced course trains aspiring mold inspectors, home inspectors, environmental consultants, and remediation professionals in IAC2-aligned inspection methodology, sampling, lab-result interpretation, and report writing. It awards a certificate of completion valid 36 months. Neither EPA nor OSHA regulates indoor mold or sets sampling thresholds; this course explains how inspectors operate within that gap. It is not an EPA certification or OSHA certification, and does not itself guarantee IAC2 accreditation of the individual. Training is delivered through HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC.

Who It's For

Built for Inspection & Remediation Professionals

Take This Course If You're

A residential/commercial home inspector adding mold services, an environmental health & safety professional, a facility or building manager, a remediation contractor, or a real estate professional entering mold inspection.

Consider Another Course If You're

General staff needing basics (see Mold Awareness Training), or a property manager needing operational-level training (see Property Management Mold Training).

Not a Substitute For

Third-party professional certifications such as IICRC AMRT, NORMI CMA/CMR, or ACAC CMR.

Learning Objectives

What You'll Be Able to Do

Understand mold characteristics and health effects, and comprehend the IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards of Practice.

Perform a visual examination of a building to identify mold and the conditions required for growth.

Use the IAC2 Mold Sampling Decision Chart and understand proper use of tools, equipment, and PPE.

Develop hypotheses, apply building-science and moisture knowledge, and use sampling devices and procedures correctly.

Interpret laboratory results and write clear, professional, defensible inspection reports.

Understand mold remediation techniques, best practices, and preventive strategies.

Curriculum

8 Modules · 22 Lessons

Taken in sequence, followed by a final examination.

  1. 01

    Module 1: IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards

    Types of Mold Inspections · IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards. Structure, purpose, and application of the Standards, and inspector responsibilities.

  2. 02

    Module 2: Understanding Mold and Mold Hazards

    What is Mold? · Negative Health Effects and Mold. Mycotoxins, ODTS, Hypersensitivity Pneumonitis, and PPE.

  3. 03

    Module 3: Exploring Mold Growth

    What Mold Needs to Grow · Building Science and Mold · Finding Mold in Buildings.

  4. 04

    Module 4: Mold Inspection Tools and PPE

    Mold Inspection Tools · Sampling Devices · Personal Protective Equipment.

  5. 05

    Module 5: Mold Sampling Procedures

    Hypotheses · Air Sampling · Surface Sampling.

  6. 06

    Module 6: Mold Inspection and Reporting

    Steps of a Mold Inspection · Interpretation of Lab Results · Mold Inspection Report.

  7. 07

    Module 7: Mold Containment, Remediation, and Prevention

    Mold Containment Methods · Mold Remediation · Remediation Guidelines · Remediation Clearance Inspection · Preventing Mold Growth.

  8. 08

    Module 8: Mold Inspection Documents

    Vital Documents mold inspectors must maintain for thorough, professional assessments.

  9. ✓

    Final Examination

    A final knowledge check must be passed to receive your certificate of completion.

Course Facts

Standards, Format & Certificate

Standards Alignment

Aligned with IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards of Practice. No federal OSHA, EPA, or CDC threshold standards exist for airborne mold; the course explains how inspectors operate within that regulatory gap.

Exam

A final examination follows the 8 modules. You must pass it to receive your certificate.

Certificate

Certificate of Completion, downloadable PDF, issued immediately on passing. Valid 36 months before a refresher is recommended.

Format

100% online, self-paced, with audio narration. Available in English and Spanish via an in-course toggle.
