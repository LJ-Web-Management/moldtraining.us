AI Agents · WebMCP

Works With Your AI Assistant

moldtraining.us supports WebMCP, so AI assistants in your browser can look up courses, compare roles, price out seats, and set up your checkout, without guessing from the page.

What Is WebMCP?

WebMCP is an emerging web standard that lets a website tell AI agents running in your browser exactly which actions it supports. Instead of an assistant reading and clicking through pages on its own, it calls the site's published tools directly and gets accurate, structured answers. You can read more in Chrome's WebMCP documentation.

What an AI Agent Can Do Here

ToolWhat it does
list_coursesLists all three courses with length, price per seat, who each course is for, topics, CEUs, and certificate validity.
recommend_courseRecommends the right course from a person's role (general staff, property management, or inspector), the same logic as our course comparison.
get_price_quoteCalculates the total for any number of seats, including the automatic bulk seat discount.
search_site_contentSearches our FAQ answers, course details, and policies.
get_certificate_verification_infoExplains how an employer can verify a certificate and links to the verification tool.
start_checkoutOpens checkout with the course and seat count already selected.

What Always Stays With You

  • Payment: an AI agent can open checkout, but it can't pay. You enter your own card details and click Pay yourself.
  • Review: when an assistant sets up your checkout, the page shows a note asking you to confirm the course and seat count before paying.
  • Your data: the tools only read public course information. They don't collect or store any personal information.

How to Try It

Use a browser or AI assistant that supports WebMCP, open moldtraining.us, and ask something like "Which mold course does my maintenance team need?" or "What would 15 seats of the Property Management course cost?". If your browser doesn't support WebMCP yet, the site works exactly as usual.

Other Resources for AI Systems

We also publish a plain-text site summary at llms.txt, a full content version at llms-full.txt, and a read-only content API described in openapi.json.

WebMCP is an emerging standard and browser support is still rolling out. AI assistants can make mistakes, so always confirm course requirements with your employer and the state mold training requirements that apply to your work.
