At a glance → This 4-hour, self-paced course trains property management teams to identify, prevent, and respond to mold, covering moisture sources, remediation basics, IAC2-aligned inspection concepts, and tenant documentation. It awards a certificate of completion valid 24 months. It is aligned with Federal OSHA, EPA, and IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards of Practice, but is not a state license, EPA certification, or OSHA certification. Training is delivered through HAZWOPER OSHA Training, LLC.

Who It's For

Built for Property Teams

Take This Course If You're

A property manager, landlord, maintenance/janitorial staff member, building inspector, leasing agent, HOA or community manager, or real estate professional handling mold-related tenant issues.

Consider Another Course If You're

General staff who just need basics (see Mold Awareness Training), or planning professional inspections requiring full sampling/reporting skills (see Mold Inspector Certification Training).

Not a Substitute For

State-required mold assessor or remediation licensing. Professionals seeking a nationally recognized remediation credential may also consider IICRC AMRT, NORMI, or ACAC certifications.

Learning Objectives

What You'll Be Able to Do

Define mold, its role in the environment, and the conditions that promote growth; identify moisture sources and apply preventive measures.

Recognize health risks associated with mold exposure and their impact on occupants.

Understand OSHA, EPA, IICRC S520-2024, and AIHA guidance as it applies to property management.

Conduct basic mold inspections, differentiate air vs. surface sampling, and identify when sampling is warranted.

Develop remediation plans, implement containment/cleanup procedures, and select appropriate PPE.

Address tenant complaints, maintain thorough documentation, manage liability risk, and set long-term prevention measures.

Curriculum

4 Modules · 12 Lessons

Taken in sequence, followed by a final examination.

  1. 01

    Module 1: Understanding Mold and Its Impacts

    What is Mold? · Moisture Problems and Preventive Maintenance · Building Science and Mold. Fundamentals of mold, common contamination sources, and health risks.

  2. 02

    Module 2: Guidelines, Inspection, and Assessment

    Regulatory Overview · Types of Mold Inspections · IAC2 Mold Inspection Standards · Mold Sampling Procedures. Covers OSHA/EPA regulations, IICRC S520-2024, and AIHA recommendations.

  3. 03

    Module 3: Mold Cleanup and Remediation

    Building Investigation and Remediation Plan · Mold Cleanup and Containment · Small vs. Large-Scale Mold Remediation.

  4. 04

    Module 4: Tenant Management and Long-Term Mold Prevention

    Tenant Complaints and Liability · Long-Term Mold Prevention Strategies.

  5. ✓

    Final Examination

    A final knowledge check must be passed to receive your certificate of completion.
