Who We Are

A Training Site Built Around One Job: Mold

We built three focused courses instead of one generic one, because a leasing agent, a maintenance supervisor, and an aspiring mold inspector don't need the same three hours of content.

Role-Specific Courses

Three courses built for three different jobs: awareness for general staff, an operational course for property teams, and an IAC2-aligned course for aspiring inspectors.

Straight Talk on Credentials

We tell you exactly what a certificate of completion is, and isn't, before you pay for a course. No overselling a credential we don't control.

Built for Busy Teams

Self-paced, mobile-friendly, and available in English and Spanish, so training fits around real schedules instead of the other way around.

Mission

Practical Training, Honestly Described

Our mission is to give property teams and mold professionals practical, standards-aligned training they can actually use, while being straightforward about what a certificate of completion is and isn't. We'd rather you enroll knowing exactly what you're getting than oversell a credential we don't control.
