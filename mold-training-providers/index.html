Buyer's Guide

How to Choose a Mold Training Provider

Mold training providers vary widely in accreditation, pricing transparency, format, and support. Here's what to check before you enroll a team, and where moldtraining.us stands on each point.

See the Checklist See Our Courses

Short answer → Before enrolling with any mold training provider, verify accreditation independently, confirm pricing is published (not "request a quote"), check certificate turnaround time, confirm which languages are supported, and read reviews on a third-party platform rather than only the provider's own testimonials page. We publish all of this upfront; see the checklist below.

The Checklist

8 Things to Check Before You Enroll

General criteria that apply to any mold training provider you're evaluating, not just us.

1. Independent Accreditation

Is the provider (or its training partner) accredited by a recognized body like IACET? Verify it on the accreditor's own directory, not the provider's claim.

2. Upfront Pricing

Is per-seat pricing published, or do you have to submit a form and wait for a callback just to see a number?

3. Certificate Turnaround

Do you get your certificate immediately after passing, or does it take days to process and mail?

4. Language Support

Is the course available only in English, or does it support a bilingual workforce?

5. Refund Policy

Is there a published refund/cancellation policy, or is that only discussed after you've already paid?

6. Independent Reviews

Can you read reviews on Google or Trustpilot, or only curated testimonials on the provider's own site?

7. Corporate/SCORM Delivery

If you're training a team, can content be deployed into your own LMS, or are you locked into their platform only?

8. Contract Terms

Is bulk training pay-as-you-go, or does it require a long-term commitment?
